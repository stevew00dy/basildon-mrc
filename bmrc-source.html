Introduction

Basildon Model Railway Club (BMRC) is a member of the Chiltern Model Railway Association and was founded in 1979.

During 1999 BMRC was fortunate to obtain it’s own club house which is now suitably renovated for use and is convenient and centrally located in Basildon. 

BMRC would like to welcome all prospective new members to pay us a visit and sample a friendly evening with fellow enthusiasts.

Join Us

The club prides itself in trying to cater for every modeller, from the young to the old, able to not so able and weird to wonderful!

We are happy to hear from enthusiasts with interests in gauges other than those mentioned above and also for modellers in non British origins. In fact we do already have a keen modeller with American interests and also, our twinning association with the ‘Rail Club de Meaux’ of France.
